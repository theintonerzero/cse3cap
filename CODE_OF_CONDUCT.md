# Code of conduct

This project is built by a small team of students, with a client and university staff, and is
open to anyone who reads or contributes to it. Everyone taking part is expected to make that a
respectful, constructive place to work.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and considerate, in issues, pull requests, reviews and the team's channels.
- Critique work, not people. Review comments are specific and kind, as
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) asks.
- Credit others' work, and own your mistakes.
- Respect privacy. Don't share someone's personal details, and treat any real student data as
  confidential.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or language.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Any conduct that would breach La Trobe University's policies on student conduct or academic
  integrity.

## Reporting

Raise a concern with any member of the team listed in the [README](README.md#team), or with the
subject's teaching staff. Reports are handled in confidence, and nobody who raises one in good
faith will be penalised for it. Conduct by La Trobe students is also covered by the
University's own policies and processes.
